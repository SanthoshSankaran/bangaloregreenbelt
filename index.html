What is Bangalore Green Belt?+

What property types do you cover?+

Villa plots, approved plotted layouts, gated communities with amenities, and farm or farmhouse plots. Lawnsview is our active featured project; villa and layout listings are coming soon.

Are plots convenient from Electronic City or Whitefield?+

BGB focuses on the Sarjapur belt, which is reachable by drive from Electronic City, Whitefield, and Sarjapur IT corridors. Enquire for current projects and visit logistics — we do not claim inventory inside those areas.

Why peri-urban plots near Sarjapur?+

Lower-density green surroundings, space for weekend farm homes, and connectivity to ORR and IT hubs make this belt popular for villa plot investment and Bangalore green living — invest beyond concrete without leaving the city entirely.

What is Lawnsview and the price?+

Lawnsview is a gated farm and farmhouse plot community in Mattanahalli by Good Old Days Farms — BGB's featured project. Indicative pricing from ₹60.5 lakh for ~5,400 sq.ft plots.

Is Lawnsview RERA registered?+

No — not presented as RERA-registered here. Verify title, conversion status and approvals with your lawyer before any token payment.

Can I build on a farm plot?+

Land use rules in Karnataka depend on conversion status and local norms. We discuss this on the intro call; final confirmation is between you, your lawyer, and the developer.

How do site visits work?+

After the intro call, we schedule a Saturday or Sunday visit for Lawnsview. Plan 60–90 minutes from central Bengaluru. No obligation to buy on the day.
